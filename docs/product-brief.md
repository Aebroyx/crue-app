# Product brief

## Brand

Crue is an indie sportswear label focused on running, priced and presented for a higher-end buyer. The product is running wear. The presentation borrows the restraint of brands such as Nike, Supreme, and Yeezy: few elements, strong type, product given room. Those names are references for attitude only. Crue does not reuse their marks, campaigns, or layouts.

The mark is a black hole. The wider identity is spatial: gravity, pull, depth. The line behind that image is the hybrid athlete, someone Crue pulls in. Space is the door into the brand. The shop itself stays quiet so the clothing can read as product, not as a theme park.

## Who it is for

People who buy running clothes as both equipment and culture. They expect a fast, sparse site on a phone and on a desktop, and they expect the object to feel considered.

The merchant operates from Indonesia. Who the first customers are (domestic, international, or both), and which languages the store speaks, is still open. See [open-questions.md](open-questions.md).

## What v1 has to do

- Introduce the brand with a fullscreen black-hole sequence, then show the home page.
- Sell running wear on a fully responsive site.
- Let a merchant run the business from Shopify Admin: products, inventory, orders, customers, discounts, pages, and the content entries the storefront reads.
- Take payment and send shipment mail through Shopify and the gateway connected there.
- Support a newsletter through Shopify Email until a later requirement says otherwise.

## What v1 does not do

- A custom admin dashboard.
- A custom backend for catalog, cart, checkout, or payments.
- A second CMS.
- A live 3D black hole. The intro is a video. A 3D scene can be specified later if the video is not enough.
- Accounts, raffles, drops, loyalty, or custom courier logic, unless a later requirement adds them.

## Success for the first build

A visitor on a phone or a laptop can pass the intro, browse products that come from Shopify, and complete checkout on Shopify. A merchant can change those products and the agreed content entries without a code deploy.
